Asking price is not the rent you pay

In International City, studios were actually contracted at an average of AED 40,865 a year over the last 12 months (DLD data via Bayut TruView), against an asking average of AED 46,301. That gap — over AED 5,000 a year — is why SalarySouq shows both numbers wherever the data allows it.
